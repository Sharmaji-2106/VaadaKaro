# User guide

## Citizen

1. **Sign up** (18+). AI processing is a separate, optional tick; it can be changed later in Settings.
2. **Draft a letter** (`/draft-letter`): type or speak in Hindi, Marathi or English. You get a formal letter,
   a fact check, and **Where to send it** from the verified directory. Edit it, then **Save as PDF**.
3. **Send it yourself**, then in **My documents** click **I sent it** (office, reference, date), and later
   **I got a reply**. VaadaKaro never sends letters for you.
4. **File a complaint on VaadaKaro** (`/file-complaint`) to have a department's officer handle it. Track it with the tracking ID.
5. **Where to complain** (`/authorities`): browse official offices by department, state and district.
6. **Legal assistant** (`/ai-assistant`): general information, not legal advice.
7. **Find a lawyer**: accept the Bar Council disclaimer, choose, and request a slot. Contact details appear once the lawyer confirms. See **My consultations**.
8. **Settings › Your data**: AI consent switch, nominee, download your data, delete your account.
9. **Grievance Officer** (`/grievance`): data requests or complaints about VaadaKaro; answered within 15 days.

## Officer

- Sign in and you land on **Overview**: counts and open urgent or high-priority complaints.
- **Complaints**: only your department's. Open one to change its status (with remarks the citizen sees), request more information, or assign it.

## Lawyer

- An admin gives your account the Lawyer role. Then fill in **Public profile**: enrolment number, State Bar Council, year, areas, languages, fee. Facts only (BCI Rule 36).
- An admin checks your enrolment and adds the verified badge.
- **Consultations**: confirm or decline requests with an optional note; mark them completed afterwards.

## Admin

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Reports | Monthly complaints per department |
| Pilot metrics | The report's success measures (drafts, corrections, exports, resolution time…) |
| Users & roles | Make officers (with a department), lawyers, admins |
| Lawyer verification | Check enrolment with the State Bar Council, then verify |
| Authority directory | Add offices; tick "checked today"; recheck entries flagged after 180 days |
| Grievance Officer | Answer requests before the 15-day deadline; overdue ones are flagged |
| Data breaches | Record a breach, notify affected users, track the CERT-In (6h) and Board (72h) deadlines |
| Audit log | Who did what, when |
